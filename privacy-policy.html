Panelhand — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026
Developer: DIFTech
Contact: panelhand.support@gmail.com

Summary

Panelhand does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings (theme, units, whether you accepted the disclaimer)
  • Saved jobs and calculations (Pro)
  • Whether Panelhand Pro is unlocked

This information is stored only on your phone. The developer cannot see or access it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Purchases

Panelhand Pro is purchased through Google Play. Google processes the payment and handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Panelhand receives only confirmation that the purchase is valid. It never receives your name, email, or payment information.

Sharing and exports

If you export or share a PDF or Excel file, it goes only where you choose to send it, using apps you select. Panelhand does not send copies anywhere.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Panelhand contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party data collection tools. Google Play may provide the developer with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics through its standard developer tools. These do not identify you.

Permissions

Panelhand does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files beyond what you choose to export.

Children

Panelhand is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
